Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Ravan Web Solutions, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We follow a fair refund policy for all custom web development services.

1. Cancellation Before Development

If you cancel your project request within 24 hours of advance payment and before active design/development has started, a full refund of the advance deposit will be processed within 5 business days.

2. During Active Development

Once design work and code development have commenced, the initial advance deposit covers design time and work completed up to that point and is non-refundable.

3. Post-Launch Satisfaction

Prior to making your website live, we present a full working live demo preview link for your review. Final deployment is performed only after your full approval.
