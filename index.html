Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?
Our basic websites start from ₹2,999, business websites from ₹5,999, and e-commerce from ₹9,999. Final price depends on your specific requirements.
How long does it take to build a website?
Basic websites are delivered in 7 days. Business websites take 10–15 days. E-commerce projects may take 3–4 weeks depending on features.
What is the payment process?
We follow a simple process: 30–50% advance → development → demo → final payment → live website. Payment details are shared after we understand your requirements.
Do you provide post-delivery support?
Yes! We offer post-delivery support for bug fixes and minor updates. Extended support plans are available on request.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
Absolutely. All our websites are fully responsive and tested on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
Do you help with domain and hosting?
Yes, we can guide you through domain registration and hosting setup, or handle it for you as part of the launch process.
